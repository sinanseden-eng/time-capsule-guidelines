A Time Capsule for the Year 2226

How will humanity remember us in two centuries? Select 5–7 authentic artifacts, justify their historical significance, and use speculative deduction to bridge the gap between our era and the year 2226.

📌 Submission Requirements

  • Strict Plagiarism Policy: Every idea, quote, and background source must be cited. Zero tolerance for copying.
  • Typography & Layout: Term papers must be written in Calibri 11 pt, tidy, and submitted in a folder.
  • Deliverables: Student Name, Number, and Class clearly stated on both soft copy and hard copy dossiers.

📦 Final Product Options

  • Option A (Physical): A curated time capsule box/model containing actual items, mock-ups, or 3D replicas.
  • Option B (Digital): An interactive website, detailed presentation (PowerPoint/Keynote), or an iMovie documentary.
  • Option C (Catalogue): A formal, high-level publication in the style of a museum catalogue.

🎯 The 3 Core Justification Pillars

  • Identity & Function: What the item is and its primary purpose in our contemporary 2020s society.
  • Narrative Alignment: Why it embodies your capsule's core message (Tech, Climate, Global Culture).
  • Future Misinterpretation: How citizens of 2226 might misunderstand it using speculative language.

💡 Target Grammar Focus: Speculative Past & Modal Deductions

Future generations won't have our context. In your report and oral presentation, analyze how items could be misunderstood using advanced modal structures:

they might think... it must have been used for... future historians could interpret it as... they can't have known about... it could easily be mistaken for... it may have signified...
