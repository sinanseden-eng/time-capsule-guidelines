A Time Capsule for the Year 2226

What will people in 2226 learn about life today? Choose 5–7 items for a time capsule. Explain why they matter, how they connect to your theme, and how people in the future might misunderstand them.

📌 What to Hand In

  • Written report: Include an introduction, a section for each of your 5–7 items, a conclusion, and a source list.
  • Finished capsule: Choose one of the three formats below. Label each item clearly.
  • Presentation: Prepare a 5–10 minute talk about your theme and items. Use pictures or your capsule to support your ideas.
  • Your own work: Write in your own words. Name the sources of facts, pictures, and quotations you use.

📦 Choose Your Capsule Format

  • A box or model: Use real objects, copies, or small models. Add a label for each item.
  • A digital capsule: Make a website, slide presentation, or short video showing your items and explanations.
  • An illustrated booklet: Make a museum-style guide with a picture and explanation for each item.

🎯 Explain Each Item

  • What is it? Name the item and explain how people use it today.
  • Why choose it? Explain what it shows about life today and how it connects to your theme.
  • What might people get wrong? Give one possible misunderstanding in 2226 and explain why it could happen.
  • Will it survive? Explain how to protect it or save a copy for the future.

💡 Useful Language: Making Guesses About the Future

Use might, may, or could to describe a possible idea. Imagine someone in 2226 finding your item. What might they think it was used for?

People in 2226 might think... They may believe it was used for... They could mistake it for... This might happen because...

Example: People in 2226 might think a phone was a small mirror because its screen is shiny. I would add a picture of someone using it to explain its real purpose.

Try this too: “It might have been used for...” describes a possible past use. “It must have been used for...” shows a strong belief based on evidence. Choose the form that fits your meaning.
